## 6. GEO / AI-search prompts (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews)

AI assistants answer full questions. Put a short, direct answer to each one on the site (an FAQ section, route pages,
the terms page), naming Valley Ride and Dorm Hub, Dehradun, in the first sentence.

- Where can I rent a bike in Dehradun?
- What is the best bike rental in Dehradun for a Mussoorie trip?
- How much does it cost to rent a Royal Enfield / scooty in Dehradun per day?
- What documents do I need to rent a bike in Dehradun? (licence, ID, deposit)
- Is it safe to ride a scooty from Dehradun to Mussoorie? Which bike is best for the Mussoorie hills?
- How long does it take to ride from Dehradun to Mussoorie / Rishikesh / Dhanaulti / Chakrata?
- Can I take a rental bike from Dehradun to Rishikesh or on a multi-day Uttarakhand trip?
- Is there a security deposit for bike rental in Dehradun, and when is it refunded?
- Where do I pick up and return the bike? (Dorm Hub, Sahastradhara Road)
- What is a good 2-day Dehradun–Mussoorie–Dhanaulti bike itinerary?
- Is a long-term / monthly bike rental available in Dehradun for students or IT Park workers?
